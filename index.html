About

I am an MSc student in Information and Network Engineering at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, expecting to graduate in June 2027. I follow a 3+2 bachelor’s–master’s study pathway and received my BSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in August 2026.

My current research lies at the intersection of 3D vision, multimodal learning, and reliable AI. I am particularly interested in learning meaningful representations from incomplete 3D observations and in understanding how model failures can be detected, localized, and corrected.

Medical imaging is an important application area for my work. I aim to develop methods that generalize across data sources, use the available evidence faithfully, and produce outputs whose limitations can be assessed. I am seeking PhD opportunities starting in autumn 2027.

Research

Feb 2026 – Present

VecSetX

Compositional 3D generation from incomplete supervision

VecSetX studies conditional completion and unconditional generation of anatomical structures, learning from multiple medical imaging datasets with complementary, incomplete annotations.

My work: I extended and fine-tuned a multi-source 3D tokenizer and prototyped a conditional diffusion transformer with cross-attention. Through matched and shuffled context controls, I investigated whether the model actually uses the observed anatomy. I also built cross-source and source-holdout evaluations and analyzed geometric failure modes, separating structural consistency from patient-specific accuracy.

The project is being developed into a manuscript for submission to CVPR 2027. I am a co-first author.

Current and Planned Projects

Flow Matching for Image Reconstruction and Translation

KTH EQ2443 · Ongoing course project · Oct 2026 – Present

I am designing a U-Net velocity-field baseline for flow-matching reconstruction from perturbed images, with image-to-image translation as a further research objective.

The planned experiments examine reconstruction fidelity, flow-estimation accuracy and sampling cost, using image-quality metrics and runtime to study quality–compute trade-offs.
