About

Research

Feb 2026 – Present

VecSetX

Compositional 3D generation from incomplete supervision

VecSetX studies conditional completion and unconditional generation of anatomical structures, learning from multiple medical imaging datasets with complementary, incomplete annotations.

My work: I extended and fine-tuned a multi-source 3D tokenizer and implemented an experimental conditional diffusion transformer. Through matched and shuffled context controls, I investigated whether the model actually uses the observed anatomy. I also built cross-source and source-holdout evaluations and analyzed geometric failure modes, separating structural consistency from patient-specific accuracy.

The project is being developed into a manuscript for submission to CVPR 2027. I am a co-first author.

Research contribution · 2026

Pulse

Structural reliability and failure diagnosis in 3D predictions

Pulse studies structural reliability in 3D airway segmentation, especially failure modes that are not captured well by aggregate overlap metrics alone.

My contribution: I analyzed 50 ATM22 airway cases using a reproducible evaluation of connectivity, component-level confidence and overlap metrics. I identified cases where largest-component filtering improved overlap scores while deleting valid, high-confidence structures.

Research relevance: This finding motivates my interest in evaluating corrective interventions by both the errors they repair and the valid information they preserve—a question I want to explore further in spatial and multimodal reasoning.
